GERMAN LANGUAGE PROGRAM

A structured A1 → B2 learning journey.

The program follows a CEFR-aligned structure and develops listening, speaking, reading, writing and grammar.

A1BEGINNER

Start your German journey.

Learn the basics, build confidence and communicate in everyday situations.

  • Basic conversations
  • Introductions & greetings
  • Everyday expressions
  • Simple grammar
A2ELEMENTARY

Strengthen your communication.

Express yourself with more clarity in daily life.

  • Daily communication
  • Family & environment
  • Past & future tenses
  • Reading & writing skills
B1INTERMEDIATE

Take your German further.

Handle more situations confidently in personal and professional life.

  • Detailed conversations
  • Work & study topics
  • Connecting ideas
  • Writing & discussion skills
B2UPPER INTERMEDIATE

Achieve greater fluency.

We offer comprehensive support across every step of your journey—from mastering German language skills to guiding you through university selection, application processes, visa requirements, and seamless transition to life in Germany. We're committed to turning your study abroad dreams into reality.

We provide structured German language training across proficiency levels A1, A2, B1, and B2, designed to align with CEFR standards. Our approach combines rigorous curriculum design with personalized learning support to help you achieve your language goals.

Our program spans 5 months, thoughtfully designed to build your language foundation through progressive modules, followed by an intensive month dedicated to exam preparation and proficiency certification.

We guide you through visa documentation requirements and help organize your paperwork for a smooth application process.
